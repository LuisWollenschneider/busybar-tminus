Count down to anything: a launch, a deadline, a birthday every year, home time every day or the stand-up every Wednesday. Keep up to 20 countdowns and flip between them with the wheel. The further away the moment is, the calmer it looks; the closer it gets, the more detail it shows and the more urgent it feels.

## What you see

- **Far out:** years and months, then *in 12 days*, then days and hours. Within a day: hours and minutes.
- **The final hour:** the label steps aside and the minutes and seconds fill the whole display.
- **Colour:** cyan while there is plenty of time, amber from the first threshold and red from the second. In the red final hour the digits beat once a second.
- **The bar** along the bottom drains through the current minute, hour or day.
- **Past the moment:** the display turns red and counts the time since, beating for the first hour, with a chime and a blink of the status light at zero.
- **The back display** shows the label, the time left and the exact date and time, and in the menu what each key does.

## How to use

On the countdown:

- **Turn the wheel**: switch between your countdowns that are on. The new one's name shows briefly, and the app opens on it next time.
- **Start**: switch between the time left and the date and time it counts down to.
- **OK**: open the list of countdowns.
- **Back**: leave the app.

In the list, turn the wheel to choose and press **OK**:

- **+ ADD NEW** makes a countdown.
- On a countdown: **Edit**, **Turn off** or **Turn on**, and **Delete** (it asks first).
- **Start** goes straight back to the countdown, showing the one you are on.

Editing goes through the name, then the year, month, day, hour and minute. The wheel changes the value, **OK** moves on, **Back** goes back a step and **Start** saves. The back display shows the keys and the countdown in words.

## Once or again and again

Each of the year, month, day and hour can also be *every*, which turns a date into a repeating countdown:

- **Year:** *YEARLY*, for birthdays and holidays: every 24 December at 18:00.
- **Month:** *MONTHLY*, such as payday on the 25th. A day the month does not have falls on its last day.
- **Day:** *DAILY*, or a weekday from *MON* to *SUN*: every day at 17:00, or every Wednesday at 9:00.
- **Hour:** *HOURLY*, at a set minute past every hour.

The finest *every* decides: choosing *WED* for the day makes the year and month irrelevant, so they disappear from the editor. After each moment a repeating countdown counts up in red for a short while, a day for yearly and monthly ones and a minute for hourly ones, then counts down to the next one.

## Settings

- **Amber from** and **Red from**: how much time is left when the colour changes.
- **Sound at zero**: the chime when the moment arrives.
- **Labels**: the names to pick from when you name a countdown, separated by commas. It starts with common ones such as *BIRTHDAY, LAUNCH, HOME TIME*: add your own, such as *MUM* or *DEMO DAY*, and remove the ones you never use. *No label* is always there.

Your countdowns are kept on the bar. Works offline.
